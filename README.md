# Customer-Service-Chatbot
A customer service chatbot is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to interact with customers, answer their questions, resolve common issues, and provide support through text or voice conversations. It operates 24/7, offering instant responses to improve customer satisfaction while reducing the workload on human support agents.
Key features of a customer service chatbot include:
Answering frequently asked questions (FAQs)
Assisting with order tracking and account inquiries
Troubleshooting common problems
Handling appointment bookings or cancellations
Escalating complex issues to human agents when necessary
Supporting multiple languages and communication channels, such as websites, mobile apps, and messaging platforms
Customer service chatbots use technologies such as natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML) to understand customer requests, provide relevant responses, and improve over time based on interactions.
Overall, a customer service chatbot helps businesses deliver faster, more efficient, and consistent customer support while enhancing the overall customer experience.
